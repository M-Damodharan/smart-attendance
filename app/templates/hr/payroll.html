💡 Company Salary Calculation Policy (30-Day Basis & 2-Day Paid Leave Quota)
• Per-Day Salary: Basic Salary ÷ 30 Days (e.g. ₹25,000 ÷ 30 = ₹833.33/day).
• Paid Leave Allowance: 2 Days/month. Approved leaves up to 2 days are never deducted.
• Unpaid Leave: Any absence beyond 2 paid leave days is deducted at the exact per-day rate.
• Sunday Working & Overtime: Calculated and added to Gross Salary before final approval.
