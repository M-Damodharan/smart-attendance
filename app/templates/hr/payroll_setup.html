💡 Standard HR Salary Configuration Guidelines
• Monthly Basic Salary: Base monthly pay (e.g. ₹25,000.00).
• Paid Leave Quota: Number of monthly paid leaves allowed (Default: 2.0 Days). Leaves beyond quota become unpaid.
• Calculation Days: Standard denominator for per-day rate (Default: 30 Days).
• OT & Sunday Rates: Leave at 0.0 to automatically derive from standard formula (1.5x regular rate) or enter fixed override rates.
